# Subscription Plans & Monetization Strategy

## Overview

The Chatbot Platform offers tiered subscription plans designed to serve different customer segments from individual developers to enterprise organizations. Our pricing strategy balances accessibility with sustainable growth.

## Subscription Tiers

### Free Trial
- **Duration**: 14 days
- **Features**: Full access to all features
- **Limitations**: None during trial period
- **Requirements**: Email verification only
- **Purpose**: Allow users to fully evaluate the platform

### Starter Plan - $29/month
**Target**: Small businesses, individual developers, startups

**Included Features:**
- Up to 3 active chatbots
- 1,000 conversations per month
- Basic analytics dashboard
- Email support
- Web widget integration
- Basic customization options
- Knowledge base (up to 100 entries)
- Standard response templates

**Limitations:**
- No API access
- No custom integrations
- No advanced analytics
- No team collaboration features
- No white-label options

### Professional Plan - $99/month
**Target**: Growing businesses, agencies, medium-sized companies

**Included Features:**
- Up to 10 active chatbots
- 10,000 conversations per month
- Advanced analytics and reporting
- Priority email and chat support
- Full API access
- Custom integrations (Slack, Teams, etc.)
- Advanced customization options
- Knowledge base (up to 1,000 entries)
- A/B testing capabilities
- Team collaboration (up to 5 users)
- Custom branding options

**Additional Benefits:**
- 99.9% uptime SLA
- Advanced conversation flows
- Multi-language support
- Export capabilities
- Webhook support

### Enterprise Plan - Custom Pricing
**Target**: Large corporations, government, high-volume users

**Included Features:**
- Unlimited chatbots
- Unlimited conversations
- Enterprise analytics and reporting
- Dedicated account manager
- Phone and priority support
- Custom integrations and development
- Advanced security features
- White-label solutions
- Unlimited team members
- Custom SLA agreements
- On-premise deployment options
- Advanced compliance features (HIPAA, SOC2)

**Custom Options:**
- Custom development
- Professional services
- Training and onboarding
- Dedicated infrastructure
- Custom data retention policies

## Usage-Based Pricing

### Overage Charges
- **Conversations**: $0.10 per additional conversation beyond plan limit
- **API Calls**: $0.01 per 100 API calls beyond included amount
- **Storage**: $0.50 per GB per month for additional knowledge base storage

### Volume Discounts
- **Annual Payment**: 20% discount on all plans
- **Multi-year Contracts**: Up to 30% discount (Enterprise only)
- **Non-profit Organizations**: 50% discount on Professional plan

## Payment Processing

### Accepted Payment Methods
- **Credit Cards**: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover
- **Digital Wallets**: PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay
- **Bank Transfer**: Available for Enterprise customers
- **Purchase Orders**: Enterprise customers only

### Billing Cycle
- **Monthly**: Charged on the same date each month
- **Annual**: Charged annually with 20% discount
- **Enterprise**: Custom billing terms available

### Currency Support
- **Primary**: USD
- **Additional**: EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD (Enterprise only)

## Customer Lifecycle Management

### Trial to Paid Conversion
- **Day 7**: Feature usage reminder email
- **Day 10**: Success stories and case studies
- **Day 13**: Final reminder with special offer
- **Day 14**: Trial expiration with easy upgrade path

### Retention Strategies
- **Usage Monitoring**: Proactive outreach for low usage
- **Feature Adoption**: Guided onboarding for advanced features
- **Success Metrics**: Regular check-ins on goal achievement
- **Loyalty Programs**: Discounts for long-term customers

- **Automatic Notifications**: When approaching plan limits
- **Seamless Upgrade**: One-click upgrade process
- **Prorated Billing**: Fair billing for mid-cycle upgrades
- **Feature Previews**: Temporary access to higher-tier features
