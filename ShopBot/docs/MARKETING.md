# ShopBot MVP - Marketing Assets & Launch Strategy

## Product Positioning

**ShopBot** is an AI-powered customer support automation platform that transforms e-commerce customer service by providing 24/7 intelligent assistance, reducing support costs by up to 70%, and improving customer satisfaction through instant responses.

### Value Proposition
- **Instant Support**: 24/7 automated customer service with sub-2-second response times
- **Cost Reduction**: Reduce support costs by 70% while handling 10x more inquiries
- **Smart Escalation**: AI determines when human intervention is needed
- **Multi-Platform**: Works seamlessly with Shopify and WooCommerce stores
- **Easy Setup**: Deploy in under 30 minutes with zero coding required

## Target Audience

### Primary Market
- **E-commerce Store Owners** (1-50 employees)
  - Annual revenue: $100K - $10M
  - Monthly support tickets: 100-5,000
  - Current pain: Overwhelmed by customer inquiries, high support costs

### Secondary Market
- **Digital Agencies** managing multiple e-commerce clients
- **Enterprise E-commerce** looking to scale support operations
- **SaaS Companies** with e-commerce components
