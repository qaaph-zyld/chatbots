Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free trial work?
You get 14 days free access to all features of your chosen plan. No credit card required to start. Cancel anytime during the trial period.
What happens if I exceed my conversation limit?
We'll notify you when you're approaching your limit. You can upgrade your plan or purchase additional conversations. Your chatbot will continue working, but you'll be charged for overages.
Can I change my plan anytime?
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate your billing accordingly.
Do you offer custom solutions?
Absolutely! Our Enterprise plan includes custom integrations and AI training. Contact our sales team to discuss your specific requirements.
Is there a setup fee?
No setup fees for Starter and Professional plans. Enterprise customers may have implementation costs depending on customization requirements.
