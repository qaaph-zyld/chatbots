## Store Owner Interview Framework

### Pre-Implementation Interview (Baseline)
**Timing**: Before ShopBot installation
**Duration**: 15-20 minutes
**Format**: Video call (recorded with permission)

#### Questions:
1. **Business Overview**
   - Tell me about your store and what you sell
   - How many orders do you process monthly?
   - What's your team size for customer support?

2. **Current Challenges**
   - How much time do you spend on customer support daily?
   - What are your biggest customer service pain points?
   - What types of questions do customers ask most?
   - How do you currently handle after-hours inquiries?

3. **Cost Analysis**
   - What's your monthly customer support cost?
   - How much revenue do you lose to poor customer experience?
   - What tools do you currently use for customer service?

4. **Goals & Expectations**
   - What would ideal customer support look like for your store?
   - How much time would you like to save daily?
   - What ROI would make this worthwhile?

### Post-Implementation Interview (Results)
**Timing**: After 2-3 weeks of ShopBot usage
**Duration**: 20-30 minutes
**Format**: Video call (recorded for testimonial)

#### Questions:
1. **Implementation Experience**
   - How was the ShopBot setup process?
   - How long did it take to see results?
   - What surprised you most about ShopBot?

2. **Performance Results**
   - How much time does ShopBot save you daily?
   - What percentage of inquiries does it handle automatically?
   - How accurate are the automated responses?
   - How has customer satisfaction changed?

3. **Business Impact**
   - What's your calculated ROI so far?
   - How has this changed your daily routine?
   - What can you focus on now instead of customer support?
   - Any unexpected benefits?

4. **Testimonial Questions**
   - Would you recommend ShopBot to other store owners?
   - What would you tell someone considering ShopBot?
   - Rate ShopBot 1-10 and explain why
   - Can we use your store as a reference?
