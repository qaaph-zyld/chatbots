# Quick Start Guide - Chatbot Platform

## Get Your First Chatbot Running in 10 Minutes

This guide will help you create and deploy your first chatbot quickly. For detailed information, refer to the [User Guide](USER_GUIDE.md).

### Step 1: Sign Up (2 minutes)

1. Go to [https://chatbot-platform.com](https://chatbot-platform.com)
2. Click "Start Free Trial"
3. Enter your email, name, and password
4. Verify your email address
5. Complete the onboarding survey

### Step 2: Create Your First Chatbot (3 minutes)

1. Click "Create New Chatbot" on your dashboard
2. Choose a template:
   - **Customer Support**: For handling common questions
   - **Lead Generation**: For capturing and qualifying leads
   - **FAQ Bot**: For answering frequently asked questions
   - **Blank**: Start from scratch
3. Name your chatbot (e.g., "Customer Support Bot")
4. Select your primary language
5. Click "Create Chatbot"

### Step 3: Customize Basic Responses (3 minutes)

1. In the chatbot editor, set up:
   - **Welcome Message**: "Hi! How can I help you today?"
   - **Fallback Response**: "I'm not sure about that. Can you rephrase your question?"
   - **Goodbye Message**: "Thanks for chatting! Have a great day!"

2. Add 3-5 basic Q&A pairs:
   - Q: "What are your hours?" A: "We're open Monday-Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM EST"
   - Q: "How can I contact support?" A: "You can reach us at support@company.com or call 1-800-123-4567"
   - Q: "What services do you offer?" A: "We provide [list your main services]"

3. Click "Save Changes"

### Step 4: Test Your Chatbot (1 minute)

1. Click the "Test" button in the editor
2. Try asking the questions you configured
3. Test the fallback response with an unrecognized question
4. Adjust responses if needed

### Step 5: Deploy Your Chatbot (1 minute)

#### For Website Integration:
1. Go to the "Deploy" tab
2. Click "Web Widget"
3. Customize the appearance (colors, position, size)
4. Copy the embed code
5. Paste it into your website's HTML before the closing `</body>` tag

#### For Testing Only:
1. Use the "Share Link" option
2. Copy the public URL
3. Share with team members for testing

## Common Quick Fixes

**Chatbot not responding correctly?**
- Check that your Q&A pairs are saved
- Ensure questions are phrased naturally
- Add alternative phrasings for the same question

**Widget not showing on website?**
- Verify the embed code is placed correctly
- Check that JavaScript is enabled
- Ensure there are no console errors

**Need help?**
- Use the in-app chat support (bottom right corner)
- Check the [User Guide](USER_GUIDE.md) for detailed instructions
- Visit our [Community Forum](https://community.chatbot-platform.com)

## Success Metrics to Track

After your first week, monitor these key metrics:
- **Response Rate**: Percentage of questions your bot can answer
- **User Satisfaction**: Ratings from chat interactions
- **Conversation Completion**: How many conversations reach their goal
- **Popular Questions**: What users ask most frequently
